# SafeSignal — AI Workplace Safety System

Workplace safety early-warning system: every incident report is classified in
seconds, the right departments are alerted over SMS, and corrective actions
are tracked through to resolution.

- **Dual AI assessment** — Groq LLM rates incident severity *and* SIF
  (Serious Injury/Fatality) potential independently, in under 2 seconds
- **Human review overrides** — HSE reviewers can overrule the AI with a
  required reason; disagreements stay visible in an AI-vs-human comparison
- **Instant SMS alerts** — high-risk reports route to mapped departments
- **Multilingual + voice input** — Hindi, Marathi, English, Hinglish with
  auto-detect and translation; offline-first via IndexedDB
- **Dashboards, audit logs, standards mapping** — Oil Mines Regulations 2017,
  OISD, Mines Act and more

**Tech:** Next.js 16, TypeScript, PostgreSQL (Neon), Recharts.
