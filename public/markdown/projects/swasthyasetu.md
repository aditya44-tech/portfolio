# SwasthyaSetu — Rural AI Doctor

AI-powered rural healthcare platform bridging the care gap: a digital
assistant for ASHA workers on the ground and a triage dashboard for doctors
at primary and district centers.

- **Multilingual NLP triage** — symptom parsing in English, Hindi and Marathi
  with duration extraction and age/pregnancy-aware risk levels
- **Smart escalation** — low risk gets home-monitoring protocols, critical
  cases route straight to the doctor dashboard
- **Health scheme matcher** — instant eligibility alerts (JSY, NTEP, ...)
- **Two portals** — mobile-first ASHA intake app plus a clinical command
  center with patient history timelines

**Tech:** TypeScript, full-stack web app with NLP and AI diagnostics.
