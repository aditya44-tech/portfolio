# Sentinel — AI Dropout Prediction Platform

Full-stack web app that helps college mentors detect, understand and intervene
with students at risk of dropping out — before it happens.

- **Deterministic risk engine** — attendance (overall + subject-wise), grades,
  backlogs, fee status and engagement collapse into a 0–100 risk score
- **AI narratives** — human-readable explanations of *why* a student is at risk
- **Intervention tracking** — mentor actions and outcomes tracked over time
- **CSV bulk import** with a documented upload format and demo dataset

Built for **Hack2Ignite 2026**.

**Tech:** TypeScript, full-stack web app with AI integration.
